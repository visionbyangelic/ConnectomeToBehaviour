The loop itself: 100 ms chunks on both sides, the two pinned environments bridged as processes speaking a JSON-lines contract that both sides' tests pin. Brain chunk (sugar at 200 Hz throughout; JON on or off per the sensory state) → pooled aDN spike count → my toy decoder (an exponential filter, τ = 0.2 s, into a clipped ramp from 2 to 40 Hz, out as a symmetric two-vector command — symmetric because the extracted sets hold no right-side descending neuron, so any left/right difference would be invented) → the audited Part 1b body pathway → leg contact transitions watched at every physics step, switching the JON drive on for the next brain chunk if the body moved. The decoder is a toy and says so everywhere; its constants were chosen before the run and not retuned after it. The decoder-only sanity run came first, as the plan requires: synthetic rates give zero command at zero input, a graded half-speed walk at the ramp's midpoint, full walking at saturation, and a halt on the audit's own timescale when the command is removed — decoder and body behaviour, documented separately so it can never be confused with brain behaviour.
