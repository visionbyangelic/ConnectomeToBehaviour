w-reset test, run 23 Nov 2026 with the pinned brain env (Brian2 2.9.0).
Script: w_reset_test.py (this folder). Raw outcome:

- Variant A (upstream's exact shape: reset 'v = v_rst; w = 0; g = 0 * mV',
  synapses with a 'w' variable present): NO error, no warning about `w`
  (only unrelated pyparsing deprecation notices from Brian2 internals).
  The neuron group does NOT have a variable named 'w' afterwards.
  Spikes: 44 total (driven neuron 30, downstream 14).
- Variant B (reset without the clause): identical spike train to A under the
  same seed -- 44 spikes, same neurons, same times.
- Variant C (clause present, no synapses at all): also no error; driven
  neuron fired its 30 spikes; still no 'w' variable on the group.

Verdict: in Brian2 2.9.0 the `w = 0` clause in the reset string is silently
ignored -- it neither errors, nor creates a variable, nor changes any spike.
Our reset therefore omits it, with dynamics identical to upstream's.
