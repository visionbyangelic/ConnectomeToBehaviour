## A. §4.3 — the dead-zone fix (replaces the θ_ext definitions)

The extension extreme θ_ext(j) is, for every proboscis DoF, the **achievable extreme**: the clamped-map achieved angle when the servo command sits at the published range limit in the extension direction — rostrum pitch **−0.954 rad**, haustellum pitch **−1.223 rad**, labrum pitch L/R **+0.2625 rad** (map values; the reach-curve calibration measured −0.9550 / −1.2237 / 0.2623–0.2624). Haustellum yaw is held at its rest value and has no θ_ext. The e→θ interpolation spans θ_rest → θ_ext, so every e in [0, 1] names a distinct intended angle inside the achievable envelope, and the feedforward command for θ_ext is exactly the range limit — no clipping occurs anywhere in the registered chain's normal operation. (The previous registration set θ_ext to margin-adjusted intended angles, −1.19 / −1.54 / 1.00, whose feedforward commands, 1.3·θ, exceed the published ranges: the achieved extension saturated at e ≈ 0.80–0.825 and the top fifth of the decoder's range was dead. E_max is unchanged — 2.1746 rad, measured — because it was always an achieved quantity; what changes is the meaning of e between 0 and 1, and r_hi at Part 5 calibration now maps to a reachable extreme.)

## B. §4.6 — substrate paragraph (replaces the Pass 6 paragraph as first applied)

The food disc sits on a **fixed pedestal**: cylinder, radius 0.5 mm, height **0.28 mm**, same centre as the disc, top surface of the disc at **T = ground + 0.33 mm**. The height follows a stated rule from a stated measurement. The reach R is defined by the reach-curve protocol (kinematics only, no food: the settled body, e swept 0→1 through the registered chain, the labrum's lowest surface point measured by MuJoCo geom-distance to the ground plane — the centre-minus-bounding-radius method is **not** used; for these elongated geoms the bounding sphere overstates the extent below the centre by ≈0.086 mm, larger than the press window). The calibration (24 Nov 2026, `REACH-CURVE-CALIBRATION.md`) measured **R = 0.3083 mm** quasi-statically; execution #3's faster ramp protocol read ≈0.296 mm; the bracket [0.296, 0.308] is recorded. **Rule: T is the smallest multiple of 0.01 mm that is ≥ R + 0.02 mm** — 0.3083 + 0.02 = 0.3283 → **T = 0.33 mm** — giving a press at e = 1 of 0.022 mm (quasi-static R) to 0.034 mm (ramp R), inside the panel's bound R ≤ T ≤ R + 0.05 under both readings. **Decision rule (pre-registered):** the contact-onset fraction f_c(T) — the achieved E/E_max at which the descending labrum surface crosses T, from the reach curve — is **0.884** at T = 0.33, the conservative (latest-onset) reading; since f_c ≤ 0.95, neither the window's upper end nor the rescope triggers. (Had f_c exceeded 0.95, T would move to R + 0.05 and f_c be recomputed; had it still exceeded 0.95, the geometry would offer no usable graded range and the Part 1b rescope would apply.) Plausibility cap: the pedestal height (0.28 mm) is below the panel's 0.6 mm cap (≈ a quarter of the 2.34 mm standing thorax height). **Sensitivity (from the same curve, no extra runs):** f_c = 0.948 / 0.901 / 0.858 / 0.816 at T = 0.316 / 0.326 / 0.336 / 0.346 mm; onset at E/E_max = 0.60 / 0.80 / 0.95 would need T = 0.405 / 0.350 / 0.316 mm. **Labelling:** the pedestal is an engineered arena parameter (class D). Every claim made from the feeding configuration states that this body, as registered with the neck held, cannot contact ground-level food, and that feeding is demonstrated on a raised substrate. The amendment is **one-time**: if Q5 fails under it, no further substrate change is permitted [one-shot consequence: see the panel question — Reviewer 1's wording makes the Part 1b rescope automatic; Reviewers 2+3's wording returns the choice between chain amendment and rescope to the panel].
